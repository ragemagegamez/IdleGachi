STORY

Forty years ago, an archaeologist unearthed a strange device. The moment he held it, visions of monsters and magical mysteries flooded his mind. Then the device flickered to life, and a tiny digital monster blinked up at him from its screen.

Back at the university, he shared his discovery with his colleagues. One of them grew jealous and stole the device. Alone in his lab, the thief took it apart and set out to build a copy. He succeeded, but his replica refused to work. Then, late one night, while tinkering with its insides, he nicked his finger. A single drop of blood fell into the machinery. The cogs and gears began to turn and spin in perfect sync, and the first Gachivice was born.

Over the next four decades, IdleGachi, the “game”, flooded the world’s markets and became a worldwide phenomenon.

Your story begins on an ordinary day at the park, when you nicked your finger on a raspberry bush and your Gachivice drank in your life force. Your Gachimon came to life, and so did your story.

Now the Gachiverse needs saving, and it falls to you to take down The Final Maw: an Abyssal Demon born from the very blood of the thief at the university.

So go. Raise your Gachimon, and become the hero you were destined to be.
